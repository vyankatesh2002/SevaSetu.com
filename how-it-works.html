Process

How SevaSetu Works

A structured workflow that takes a civic issue from report to confirmation.

📝 Report an Issue
01

📝 Report

A citizen reports a civic issue with text, image, voice or location.

02

💡 Understand

The issue is captured and organised so it can be clearly understood.

03

🗂️ Organize

The complaint is structured with category, priority and details.

04

🧭 Route

It is routed to the relevant service area for review.

05

🔎 Review

An assigned officer reviews and acts on the complaint.

06

⚙️ Resolve

Work progresses with status updates and proof.

07

📈 Track

The citizen can track the full journey at any time.

08

✅ Confirm

The citizen confirms resolution and provides feedback.
