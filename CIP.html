Process

How SevaSetu Works

A simple, five-step process designed to make reporting and resolving civic issues straightforward and transparent.

01
📝

Report

Describe the issue using text, voice, image, or location.

02
🧠

Understand

SevaSetu assists in understanding and organizing the submitted grievance.

03
🔀

Route

The grievance is directed toward the appropriate service area or authority.

04
🛠️

Resolve

The responsible authority reviews and works on the issue.

05
📈

Track

Citizens can follow progress and receive updates until resolution.

🤖 AI assists the workflow; final administrative decisions remain with authorized personnel.
