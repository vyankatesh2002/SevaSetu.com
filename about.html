About SevaSetu

Building a More Connected Civic Experience

SevaSetu is an AI-assisted digital grievance management research prototype designed to simplify civic issue reporting and improve transparency.

📝 Report an Issue 🔍 Track Complaint

Why SevaSetu

The Problem We're Exploring

Civic grievance reporting is often fragmented, unclear and hard to track. SevaSetu explores how a citizen-centred, AI-assisted workflow can help.

🎯 Citizen-Centered Approach

Simplifies reporting with text, voice, image and location inputs, in multiple languages.

🤖 AI-Assisted Workflow

Helps organise, categorise and route grievances for faster, clearer review.

🧭 Transparency

Citizens can track status and see the journey of their complaint from submission onward.

♿ Accessibility

Designed with accessibility principals — keyboard navigation, resizable text, high contrast.

🛡️ Responsible AI

AI assists the workflow. Final administrative decisions remain with authorised personnel.

🔬 Research Prototype

An academic demonstration exploring smart governance — not an official government website.

Core Modules

One Platform, Connected Modules

Every part of SevaSetu shares the same design language and connected data.

👤 Citizen

Register, track and manage complaints with live status timelines.

👮 Officer

Accept, update status, upload proof, GPS verify and escalate complaints.

🏢 Department

Analytics, officers, complaints, heatmaps and exports for a service area.

🛠️ Admin

System-wide oversight — departments, citizens, officers, statistics and audit logs.
